# Introduction

## Why Orcinus at all? Isn't Dolphin good enough?

Yes, of course Dolphin is more than good enough for the vast majority of its users.

However, if, like me, you've endured decades of torment at the hands of Windows Explorer and switched to Directory Opus long ago – a file manager that plays in an entirely different league – you may find yourself missing quite a few things in Dolphin.

From the sheer density of information displayed to color-coded file types and flat views of directory contents, there is still room for improvement for power users who spend a large part of their day working in a file manager.

Orcinus was born out of this need.

Orcinus is a fork of Dolphin. A substantial portion of the application – currently around 55% of its code – is therefore still based on Dolphin. Or, if you will: dwarfs standing on the shoulders of giants. Or rather, orcas standing on the fins of dolphins. Erm … something like that.

Anyone familiar with Dolphin will feel right at home in Orcinus at first glance.

*[Image: Dolphin and Orcinus side by side in their default configurations – remarkably similar at first glance.]*

First impressions can be deceiving, however – Orcinus is not just a dolphin in a black-and-white skin. It builds upon Dolphin's stable, proven foundation, adding a wealth of useful features.

Discovering these features and learning how to put them to good use is precisely what this handbook is all about.

**Our motto:**

*Make the common case simple and excellent.*

*Make the uncommon case possible.*
